What is Cold Jump?

Cold Jump is an animated sci-fi mystery set in a near-future Kerala, where a journalist investigates a series of disappearances in a futuristic smart city. As he digs deeper, he uncovers manipulated evidence, altered memories, and a conspiracy that challenges his understanding of what really happened. The story explores truth, identity, and the fragility of memory.

How it all started

It all started simply as sort of a daring attempt on September 2023 where I was discussing with Surya and other members of Studio Mocktale about issues of copyright while dubbing videos into youtube. I don't know why but my immediate reaction was, "why not make our own original content" and that birthed Cold Jump as we see here today.

The vision was always the same and simple. An original malayalam animation inspired via different mediums(mainly anime) such that it tells a good story and it motivates others to create their own films and break standards of what malayalam animation could be and should be treated as. An original content for our Studio that could showcase our voice acting work and many more. All aspirations into a single bottle. A daring peace of design that is crazy good to work with. We don't mind if no one watches. All of it was and is and will be a crazy experience that we creatives deal with.

Project Status

We are still working on the story - refining it, hoping to deliver the audience a grand experience. After 2-3 years of constant trial and error, we started moving and we learned a LOT. We are still learning yes but we are finally ready to create something out of this. Our skills in direction, animation, storywriting and more got much better and better.

We went forward with creating a story in which the protagonist was same, an investigate journalist, in a murder mystery story that dealt with multiple topics like loneliness, isolation, confusion, etc... We are also working on concept trailers, character sheets, color script and more.
